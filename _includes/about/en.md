## About Me

My current focus is applying **Advanced Oxidation Processes (AOPs)** and **artificial intelligence (AI)** to water treatment and other environmental challenges. Part of the work is about persuading stubborn contaminants to leave the water; the other part is about using AI to modelling the water purification. In short, I am interested in drinking water purification and make the process be a little smarter.

When the experiments need a break, I am usually playing badminton, tennis, or working out, sometime might tinkering with something fun.
